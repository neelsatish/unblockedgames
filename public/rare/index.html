        HOW TO PLAY

        1. You get 7 prompts, like "Name a fruit". Each has many right answers.
        2. You have 25 seconds to type one answer. Wrong answers can be retried until time runs out.
        3. The rarer your correct answer, the more points it scores and the further you travel across campus.
        4. Misspelled? You'll be asked "Did you mean...?" Press GO again to confirm.

        Rarity comes from real popularity data (how many Wikipedias cover an answer, and how often English Wikipedia readers view it), checked by hand. It never changes based on who plays first.
