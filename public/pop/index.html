          An answer should have counted?

          HOW TO PLAY

          1. Everyone sits round the lab bench and gets the same prompt, like "Name a fruit".
          2. Whoever holds the fizzing flask must name a correct answer nobody has used for this prompt. Then the flask moves to the next seat.
          3. The flask has one hidden fuse that keeps burning as it moves. Nobody sees the time; watch how hard it fizzes. You always get at least a few seconds when it reaches you.
          4. Holding it when it pops costs a heart. Lose them all and you're out. Last player standing wins.
          5. Wrong or repeated answers cost nothing but time. Rarer answers fill your spare-heart meter faster.
          6. One emergency pass per game hands the flask on without an answer.
